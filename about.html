About The Grasse Fragrance Co.

20 years in the business.

Since 2006, The Grasse Fragrance Co. has supported Filipino entrepreneurs and businesses with fragrance oils from Grasse, France, packaging solutions, and perfumery education.

Complete perfumery resource

The complete perfumery resource in the Philippines

Established in 2006, The Grasse Fragrance Co. helps Filipino entrepreneurs and businesses in the fragrance, beauty, personal care and home care industries create perfume and fragrance products under brands of their own.

Our offer brings three essentials together, as our descriptor says: Fragrances · Packaging · Tutorials. Today, GFC serves businesses across the fragrance, cosmetics, personal care, home fragrance, and related industries from four branches: Makati, Rockwell, Fairview and Davao.

Our mission

From concept to launch

To support Filipino entrepreneurs and businesses in developing quality fragrance products through fragrance oils from Grasse, France, carefully selected packaging solutions, and practical perfumery education.

We bring essential resources and knowledge together to help our clients develop their products from concept to launch.

Our vision

The perfumery partner Filipino brands trust most

To be the perfumery partner Filipino brands trust most, and to see brands created through French expertise and Filipino creativity take their place among the world's finest.

Having helped shape Philippine perfumery for two decades, we will continue to move the industry forward through quality, authenticity, and expertise.
