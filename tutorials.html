How it works

From first visit to first bottle

  • 1Message or visit a branch. Tell us what you want to make. Our team walks you through the Starter Kit and the tutorial schedule.
  • 2Choose your fragrance direction. Smell the library and pick the fragrance oil for your first product.

  • 4Build your brand. Pick packaging, plan your pricing, and come back for fragrance oils and bottles as you grow.
Frequently asked questions

Before you start

Do I need perfume-making experience?
No. The basic tutorial introduces the fundamentals of perfume making and is suitable for beginners.
Can I choose my fragrance oil?
Our team can guide you through available fragrance options. Selection is subject to the current Starter Kit arrangements.
Can I visit a branch before purchasing?
Yes. Visit our branches to explore products and inquire about the Starter Kit and tutorials. See branches and hours.
How do I place an order?
Send us a message on Viber or Messenger, or visit a GFC branch. You can also use the inquiry form.
Already know what you need?

Ready with your shopping list?

If you already know which fragrance oils or packaging materials you need, send your requirements directly to our team. We can help you check available products and coordinate the next steps for your order.
