Celebrating 20 years: 2006 to 2026

Two decades of working with Filipino entrepreneurs, growing brands, and our longstanding French fragrance partner.

Since 2006, The Grasse Fragrance Co. has helped Filipino entrepreneurs and businesses build fragrance brands of exceptional quality. As the complete perfumery resource in the Philippines, we offer fragrance oils from Grasse, France, quality packaging, and the expertise to take every brand from concept to finished product.

Since July 2006

Our partnership with Expressions Parfumées, a French perfume composition house located in Grasse, the world capital of perfumery, began in July 2006. Since then, every fragrance oil we offer has been created in Grasse, and reaches our clients exactly as it was created, the same in every order.

20 Years of Fragrance from Grasse.

Four branches, one standard

From Makati we grew to Fairview, Davao and Rockwell. Four branches now serve our clients as equals, each with the fragrance library, the packaging range, and the Starter Kit tutorial that introduces first-time founders to perfume making.

What has not changed

  • Quality. We never compromise on what goes into a fragrance brand, from the fragrance oil to the final packaging.
  • Authenticity. Our connection to French perfumery is genuine, and we speak honestly about the origin of our fragrance oils.
  • Expertise. We understand fragrance in depth, and we share that knowledge generously.
  • Partnership. We work alongside our clients from the first sample to the finished product, and beyond.

Thank you to the clients and businesses who have been part of the GFC story. Here is to the next chapter of Filipino creativity and French expertise.
