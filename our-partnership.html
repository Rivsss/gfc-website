About Expressions Parfumées

A French perfume composition house in Grasse.

Founded by perfumers in 1982, Expressions Parfumées is a French perfume composition house based in Grasse, the world capital of perfumery. It describes itself as an Innovative Fragrance Designer.

The company develops fragrance compositions for fine fragrance, cosmetics, toiletries, air care, and home care applications. It is active in more than 50 markets, manufactures 100% in France, and has been a stand-alone entity within the Givaudan group since 2018.

Combining perfumery expertise with research, creativity, and technical knowledge, Expressions Parfumées serves businesses across international markets.

Source: epparfums.com and Givaudan. Facts checked October 2026; confirm figures with Expressions Parfumées before publishing.

Grasse, France

The world capital of perfumery

Grasse is the world capital of perfumery. In 2018, UNESCO inscribed the perfume skills of the Pays de Grasse, from cultivating perfume plants to the art of perfume composition, on its list of Intangible Cultural Heritage.

Every fragrance oil we offer is created in Grasse by Expressions Parfumées: the art of perfume composition, refined over centuries in Grasse, France.
